Featured Wineries

This Juneteenth, we're celebrating freedom in wine country. Every tasting, every vineyard, every experience on this trip has been handpicked to give you an unforgettable Napa Valley experience.

Brown Estate

A Napa Valley estate winery since 1996. Known for award-winning Zinfandels and bold reds. Tasting room in downtown Napa, walking distance from your hotel. This is where the story begins.

Visit Website → TripAdvisor →

J. Moss Wines

Boutique winery specializing in handcrafted Cabernet and Merlot. Intimate, appointment-only tastings just 10 minutes from downtown Napa. Owners James and Janet Moss share their love story with every glass.

Book Tasting → TripAdvisor →

Okapi Wines

Small-batch artisanal Cabernet Sauvignon with a mission. Tastings at The Caves at Soda Canyon, and a portion of proceeds supports okapi conservation in the Congo. Wine with purpose.

Learn More → TripAdvisor →

Housley

Owned by Tamera Mowry-Housley (Sister, Sister) and her husband Adam. Family-friendly tasting room with indoor and outdoor seating, great music, and affordable $25 tastings with chocolate pairing. Pure joy in a glass.

Learn More →

River Terrace Inn

Downtown Napa's premier riverfront hotel — your home base for the Juneteenth trip. Walking distance to the Brown Estate tasting room and Napa's best restaurants.
