Featured Black-Owned Wineries

This Juneteenth, we're centering Black excellence in wine country. Every tasting, every vineyard, every experience on this trip supports Black-owned businesses and celebrates the legacy of Black winemakers in Napa Valley.

Brown Estate

Napa Valley's first Black-owned winery (founded 1996). Known for award-winning Zinfandels and bold reds. Tasting room in downtown Napa, walking distance from your hotel. This is where the story begins.

Visit Website →

J. Moss Wines

Boutique winery specializing in handcrafted Cabernet and Merlot. Intimate, appointment-only tastings just 10 minutes from downtown Napa. Owners James and Janet Moss share their love story with every glass.

Book Tasting →

Okapi Wines

Small-batch artisanal Cabernet Sauvignon with a mission. Tastings at The Caves at Soda Canyon, and a portion of proceeds supports okapi conservation in the Congo. Wine with purpose.

Learn More →

Housely

Owned by Tamara Mowry (Sister, Sister) and her husband Adam. Family-friendly tasting room with indoor and outdoor seating, great music, and affordable $25 tastings with chocolate pairing. Pure joy in a glass.

Visit Website →

Why This Trip is Special

Juneteenth isn't just a holiday—it's a celebration of freedom, resilience, and Black excellence. And there's no better place to honor that than in Napa Valley, where Black winemakers have been breaking barriers and crafting world-class wines for decades.

This trip is intentionally curated to support Black-owned businesses at every turn. From the wineries you visit to the restaurants where you dine, from the chauffeur who drives you to the cultural experiences that frame your journey—every dollar you spend uplifts Black entrepreneurs and tells a story that deserves to be celebrated.

You'll taste incredible wines, yes. But you'll also connect with the people behind them, hear their stories, and understand the challenges they've overcome to thrive in an industry that wasn't built for them. It's wine country with purpose. It's celebration with meaning. And it's a trip you'll never forget.
